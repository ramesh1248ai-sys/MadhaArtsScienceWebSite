Training for careers, learning in the field

  • Campus to Corporate placement training for the move into work

Admissions

Who can apply

For career guidance and counselling, reach us on 81818 15379 or at madhaasc@gmail.com.

UG

Undergraduate admissions

Admission to our undergraduate courses is open to students who have passed the +2 (Higher Secondary) examinations.

Applications are made available every academic year after the results of the +2 examinations are released.

PG

Postgraduate admissions

Admission to our postgraduate course (M.Com) is based on the student’s qualifications in their undergraduate course.

Speak to the admissions team to check your eligibility before you apply.

How to apply

Three simple steps

1

Choose a programme

Compare our undergraduate and postgraduate options and visit the department pages.

2

3

Apply once applications open for the academic year.
