Photo enhancements

Every photograph on the redesigned site was restored with AI super-resolution and then colour-graded. Drag across any image to compare the original with the enhanced version.

1

AI restoration

Real-ESRGAN super-resolution removes JPEG blocking and noise and recovers edge detail. Small photos were upscaled up to 3.5×.

2

Photographic grade

White balance, local contrast, vibrance, a gentle tone curve, and a graduated filter on washed-out skies.

3

Authenticity kept

No generative face restoration, so people look exactly like themselves. Small signs were masked wherever the AI tried to invent lettering.
