About the College

A premier institution affiliated to the University of Madras, providing wholesome education to the student community since 2006–07.

Madha Arts & Science College

Two decades of wholesome education in Chennai

Madha Arts and Science College is a premier institution affiliated to the University of Madras and located in Chennai, providing wholesome education to the student community.

The institution was started in the year 2006–07 and has over the years developed into an intellectual centre of academic excellence. The college offers excellent facilities and infrastructure, coupled with experienced faculty who are committed to providing an outstanding academic experience to our students.

The institution is part of the Madha Group of Educational Institutions, founded by Dr. Ln. S. Peter.

Madha Group

A legacy in education since 1995

The college is part of the Madha Group of Educational Institutions, founded by Dr. Ln. S. Peter, an educationalist and philanthropist.

The group has founded institutions across Engineering, Medicine, Dentistry, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Management, Teacher Training and several schools under the Soosaiya Peter Educational Trust and the Loordu Ammal Educational Trust.

Management team

Leadership of the Madha Group

Founder & Chairman

Dr. Ln. S. Peter

One of the early pioneers of education in Tamil Nadu, Dr. Ln. S. Peter began life in his home town of Thirumaiyam in Pudukkottai District. His journey in education started with the establishment of the group’s Engineering College in 1995 under the Soosaiya Peter Educational Trust, and has since grown to institutions spanning Medicine, Dentistry, Physiotherapy, Nursing, Management, Arts and Sciences, and several CBSE and Matriculation schools across Chennai.

Vice-Chairperson

Ms. Mercy Florence Peter

An engineer by profession, Ms. Mercy Florence Peter completed her undergraduate degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering and a postgraduate degree (MS) in Management Information Systems at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, USA.

Managing Director

Mr. Ajay Ravindra Kumar

One of the early employees of Tesla, Inc. in California, Mr. Ajay Ravindra Kumar worked with its engineering team on the Roadster, Model S and Model X programmes. He holds a degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering, an MS in Information Technology Management from the University of Texas at Dallas, and an LLB from The Tamil Nadu Dr. Ambedkar Law University.
