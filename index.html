About the college

An intellectual centre of academic excellence

Madha Arts and Science College provides wholesome education to the student community in Chennai. Started in 2006–07, the institution has grown into a centre of academic excellence.

  • Excellent facilities and infrastructure across the campus
  • Experienced faculty committed to an outstanding academic experience
  • Part of the Madha Group of Educational Institutions, founded by Dr. Ln. S. Peter
